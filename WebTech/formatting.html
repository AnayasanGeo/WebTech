<b> (bold): Formats text in bold purely visual style.

<strong> (Strong Importance): Formats text in bold but signals that the content has a serious importance, seriousness, or urgency.

<i> (Italic): Formats texts in italics purely for visual style or a change in vocal tone.

<em> (Emphasis): Formats texts in italics but adds semantic emphasis to change the meaning of a sentence

<u>(Underline): Applies a solid underline to the text.

<mark> (Highlighted Text): Highlights text with a yellow background default.

<small> (Side Comments): Renders text one size smaller than the surrounding text. *this is a sample

<del> (Delete Text): Strikes a line through text to show it has been removed. *This is a sample.
